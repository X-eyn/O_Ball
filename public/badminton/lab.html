Try these

Flick: one finger, a quick stroke forward (up the pad).
Swing: pull back (a stroke down the pad), lift, then flick forward. The pause between is the wind-up.
Hard vs soft: the faster the flick, the harder the shot.
Sideways flick: angle the flick left or right.
Tap and double tap: one finger.
Two-finger tap: both fingers at once (a right click).
Two-finger swipe: up, down, left, right.
Pinch: in or out.
